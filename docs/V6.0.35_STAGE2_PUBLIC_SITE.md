- `holographic.html` now has separate Departments, News & Updates, and Media Gallery views.
- Published posts targeting `traditional` or `both` are loaded from `data/managed-content.json` and displayed with search, category filters, dates, featured article and nine-per-page pagination. Drafts/archived posts are hidden.

- The traditional website here means the EXISTING included holographic.html site. A distinct external repository (if any) is not automatically updated.
- Files touched: holographic.html, src/holographic.css, src/managed-content.js, new src/public-content.js, docs/VERSION.json and this note.
